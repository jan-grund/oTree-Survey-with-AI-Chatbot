Voluntary participation and anonymity

Participation in this study is voluntary. You may end your participation at any time and without giving reasons. The data and personal information collected in this study are treated confidentially. Project staff who have access to personal data through direct contact with you are bound by confidentiality. The results of the study will be published in anonymized form, that is, without your data being attributable to you as a person.

Data protection

Your data are collected without asking for your name. Your responses are stored under a randomly assigned participant code generated by the study software. This means it is not possible for project staff to link your responses to your identity. As long as this code exists, you may request deletion of the data collected from you by providing your code. Please keep your code so that you can request deletion later if you wish. The codes will be deleted after the study is completed, but no earlier than 30.09.2026. After that date, the data exist only in fully anonymized form and deletion can no longer be requested.

Your Prolific ID is used solely to administer your compensation. It is stored separately from your study responses and cannot be used to identify you in the analysis or in any publication.

Interaction with the AI chatbot

During the study, you will interact with an AI chatbot. The messages you send are processed by OpenAI, a provider based in the United States, in order to generate the chatbot's responses. This means the messages you write in the chat are transmitted to and processed on servers outside the European Union. OpenAI does not use data sent through its programming interface (API) to train its models, and such data are deleted after at most 30 days.

Because your chat messages are processed by an external provider, please do not enter any personal information about yourself or other people — such as names, addresses, contact details, or anything that could identify you or someone else. Please write only about the topic of the task.

Data storage and retention

During the study, your responses and the chat transcript are stored on a server hosted by Hetzner Online GmbH in a data center located in Germany. For the purpose of analysis, the data are transferred to and stored on local computers at the University of Duisburg-Essen and deleted no later than 31.12.2026. In the event of a publication of the research results, the data will additionally be made available in fully anonymized form on a public repository for scientific data.
