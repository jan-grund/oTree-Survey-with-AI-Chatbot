Thank you for taking part in this study.

This study investigated how different response styles of an AI system influence the way people evaluate information. For this purpose, you interacted with an AI chatbot whose answers differed in the way information was presented.

For one group of participants, the answers were formulated so that uncertainties, the limits of available knowledge, and differing perspectives were explicitly taken into account. This way of presenting information was intended to convey a form of intellectual humility in dealing with knowledge. In the other condition, the answers followed a standard, more direct response style, in which information was presented as more firmly established. You were randomly placed into one of two groups — this was done automatically and had nothing to do with your answers.

The aim of the study is to investigate whether and how such differences in the presentation of information influence the evaluation of content as well as the way people deal with uncertainty. A further part of the study examines the extent to which intellectual humility can be encouraged in the short term by an AI chatbot modeling it.

If you have any questions about the study or would like to withdraw your participation afterward, you can contact the person responsible for the project at any time:

Jan-Sebastian Grund (University of Duisburg-Essen), jan-sebastian.grund@stud.uni-due.de
